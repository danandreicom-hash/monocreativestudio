## Positioning

MONO Creative Studio brings an art shop and a print lab together under one brand. **Shop** helps people choose artwork; **Print Lab** helps people discuss printing their own work or a project. This two-door structure preserves the v0.1 direction while keeping the launch catalogue small (D-003).

Confirmed: MONO has its own print lab (D-002); Vladimir Andrei owns the business and Dan Andrei approves the site (D-001). Phase 1 includes a small Shopify shop that grows over time, without automated fulfillment (D-003). MONO's identity stays separate from its ecosystem partners; public partner mentions need permission.

Proposed positioning: a considered art selection with a clear route to professional printing. This is a communication direction, not evidence of print quality. Museum quality, archival longevity, colour fidelity, proofs, framing, production capacity and turnaround remain `[to confirm]`; none is a launch promise.

## Audiences and conversion priorities

| Audience                                       | Need to address                                                    | Entry and primary action          |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Artists and photographers                      | Discuss printing their work and editions                           | Print Lab → Request a quote       |
| Designers, architects, agencies and businesses | Discuss project quantities, dimensions and delivery needs          | Designers / B2B → Request a quote |
| Collectors and first-time buyers               | Understand the artwork, artist, actual price and available options | Shop → View work → Add to cart    |

Working hypothesis: qualified quote requests are the main early conversion; shop orders are a second path. Samples can become an introductory step only after contents, availability, price and delivery are confirmed. No volume discounts, formats or edition services are implied by an audience label.

## Voice and message hierarchy

Keep the calm, image-led v0.1 direction. Shop language is welcoming and specific about the work; lab language asks about the project and explains only confirmed services. Use short sentences and one main action per block. Avoid superiority comparisons and guarantees.

Home sequence: two-door hero → Shop / Print Lab cards → confirmed reasons to choose MONO → available works → professional audiences → samples if ready → contact. Omit blocks without evidence or content. Do not promise a regular release schedule or display invented scarcity. Edition labels, counts and certificates require the approved product model (TASK-004).

Working Home headline: “Fine art prints, made with care.” Hero actions: “Shop prints” and “Print Lab”. Lab conversion action: “Request a quote”. Browse action: “View work”. These are proposals for G2, not approved copy.
