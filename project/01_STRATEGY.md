## Master positioning

CC WEBDEV MASTER is the reusable delivery foundation for corporate/B2B websites. It standardizes collaboration and implementation without importing assumptions from a specific client.

## Objectives

- Reduce manual handoffs through small, versioned project documents.
- Make approvals, active work, implementation commits and QA traceable.
- Keep Dan in control of creative direction and material decisions.
- Give Claude Code implementation-ready specifications.
- Preserve the target of 0 EUR incremental collaboration cost.

## Principles

- Client research and positioning belong in that client's repository.
- An approved version remains authoritative until a later decision explicitly supersedes it.
- Drafts and recommendations do not authorize implementation.
- Prefer reusable defaults, but validate stack, CMS, hosting and integrations per client.

## Research and validation backlog

- Validate the boilerplate against the first separate client repository.
- Measure handoff time, ambiguity, rework and remaining copy/paste.
- Record reusable lessons here only after Dan approves their promotion to the master.
