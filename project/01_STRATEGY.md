## Positioning

MONO Creative Studio prints fine art in its own lab, for people who make art, sell art and place art in spaces. One brand, two doors:

- **Shop:** limited and open editions by selected artists, ready to hang or framed.
- **Print Lab:** professional printing for artists, photographers, designers and companies, from one print to a volume order.

Positioning line (working): the quality of a serious lab, the warmth of a gallery, a service that answers like a professional. The shop sells the art; the lab earns the trust that makes the shop credible.

Place in the ecosystem: MONO is owned and run by Vladimir Andrei. It is a partner of Arthalle (curation) and Conceptiones (art for hospitality and interiors), not a brand of either. It may be named as production partner where the client agrees; the brands keep separate voices.

## Audiences (priority order)

1. **Artists and photographers** who need editions printed with colour they can rely on. Entry: Print Lab, sample pack, quote.
2. **Designers, architects, agencies and companies** who need volume, custom sizes, framing and invoicing. Entry: Print Lab B2B, quote.
3. **Collectors and first-time art buyers.** Entry: Shop, limited editions.

Primary conversion hypothesis: a qualified quote request or a sample-pack order. The shop is populated over time (D-003) and must look curated, never empty.

## Voice

Borrowed method, not wording:

- From Conceptiones: calm, declarative, sentence-case headlines; confident without hype; image leads, text supports.
- From Creative Corporation: one sharp contrast in a headline where it earns its place; plain business language; a first conversation commits to nothing.
- From Arthalle: warm and inviting for someone buying their first piece; no gatekeeping vocabulary.

Two registers: Shop is warmer and gallery-like (edition, artist, collection); Print Lab is precise and technical (files, colour, paper, volume). Same brand, same header and footer, different sentences.

## What the three reference sites teach (see `project/source/reference-analysis-2026-10.md`)

- Concrete benefits beat superlatives; their unquantified "best" claims are exactly what MONO avoids.
- One promise and one verb per block; the Shop repeats a single invitation ("Discover" style), the Print Lab repeats "Request a quote".
- A sample pack is the entry point for new customers; professionals get their own door.
- Several ways to reach a person, shown plainly.
