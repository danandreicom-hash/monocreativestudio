## Goal

Website, Print Lab service pages and a small online shop for MONO Creative Studio (monocreativestudio.com): museum-quality fine art prints, produced in MONO's own lab.

## Entity

MONO Creative Studio is owned and managed by Vladimir Andrei. It is a partner of the Dan St. Andrei ecosystem (Arthalle, Conceptiones, Creative Corporation); it is not presented as a Dan Andrei company. Brand and communication stay separate from Arthalle and Conceptiones.

## Audiences

1. Artists and photographers who need editions printed (Print Lab).
2. Interior designers, architects, agencies and businesses ordering volume (Print Lab, B2B quote).
3. Collectors and art buyers (Shop).

## Phase 1 scope (D-003)

- Presentation site: Home, Print Lab hub and subpages (artists, designers, B2B, materials, samples), About, Journal, Contact.
- Request a Quote and Sample Pack flows.
- Small online shop on Shopify, populated over time: collection, product page, cart, checkout (Shopify-hosted).
- Bilingual RO/EN: to be decided (open question 1).

## Out of scope (phase 1)

Automated fulfillment or dropshipping, print upload configurator, customer accounts beyond Shopify defaults, marketplace for third-party artists.

## Open Questions

1. Languages: EN only, or RO and EN from launch?
2. Lab facts to confirm before any public claim: printers, papers (brands, weights), maximum size, framing and finishing offered, colour-management process, sample-pack contents, lead times.
3. Shopify: store plan and owner account; separate store from Arthalle or shared backend (decide before TASK-004).
4. Legal entity for invoicing, VAT status, shipping zones and returns policy for made-to-order prints.
5. Edition model for the Shop: edition sizes, numbering, certificate and authentication (NFC/QR) in phase 1 or later.
6. First artists and works for the Shop; rights and credits for every image.
7. Domain, email and form delivery provider (cost approvals under CC_WEBDEV section 2).
