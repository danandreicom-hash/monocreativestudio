## 1. Context & positioning

MONO Creative Studio produces museum-quality art prints for artists and companies. The online shop (Shopify) sells printed artworks B2C, with special prices and discounts for large quantities and B2B. No automated fulfillment (dropshipping) at this time.

Recommended positioning (as written in the original): WhiteWall quality, plus the gallery experience of LUMAS / YellowKorner, plus the professional service of theprintspace, under one clean brand.

Possible taglines:

- "Museum-quality art prints. For artists, for collectors, for spaces."
- „Printuri de artă de calitate muzeală. Direct de la studio.”
- „Artă limitată. Calitate de muzeu. Prețuri inteligente.”

## 2. Reference analysis (four sites studied)

| Reference     | Strengths                                                                                                  | What MONO takes                                                                   |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| theprintspace | Print on demand, white-label, certificates, Shopify integrations, Hahnemühle, sample packs, carbon neutral | Service model for artists, sample packs, white-label option, archival focus       |
| YellowKorner  | Accessible limited editions, galleries plus online, curatorial voice                                       | Accessible limited editions, seasonal collections, certificate and numbering      |
| WhiteWall     | Multi-lab, TIPA-awarded, configurator, strong B2B, Shopify app, sample packs                               | "Gallery quality" standard, B2B programme, materials and frames configurator      |
| LUMAS         | Premium art shop, signed limited editions, framing, gift ideas, artist stories                             | Online gallery experience, limited and signed, several sizes, gifts, artist pages |

Common traits of leaders: real museum quality (archival papers and pigments), limited editions with certificate (ideally signed), a gallery experience rather than a decor shop, beautiful B2C and serious B2B (volume, discounts, white-label), sample packs plus a materials/frames configurator plus strong storytelling.

## 3. Menu structure

Two zones: Zone 1 Print Lab (services for artists, designers, photographers, galleries, B2B) and Zone 2 Online Store (B2C and B2B sales).

- Shop: All Prints, New Arrivals, Bestsellers, Limited Editions, By Artist, By Theme, By Size / Price
- Print Lab: For Artists & Photographers, For Designers & Agencies, For Business (B2B), Materials & Papers, Framing Options, Order Samples
- About: Our Process, Quality Standards, Studio
- Journal
- Contact
- Header right: Search, Account, Cart, "Request a Quote" (secondary button to the B2B/volume form)
- Sticky bar proposal: "Museum-quality prints - archival pigments - Limited editions available"

## 4. Page wireframes (text)

Homepage: full-bleed hero (paper texture and detail); title "Museum-quality art prints"; subtitle "Limited editions for collectors. Professional printing for artists & businesses."; two equal CTAs "Shop Prints" and "Print Lab Services". Then two path cards (Shop the Collection / Print Lab), Bestsellers or New Arrivals (Shop only), "Why MONO" (papers, pigments, colour management, limited editions with certificate), "For Professionals" block, footer (Shop, Print Lab, Materials, Samples, B2B Quote, Contact).

Shop: collection page with filters (artist, theme, size, price, edition status), product grid with wall mockup, artist and title, "From 380 EUR" and "37/75 remaining", Limited or Open Edition badge. Product page: gallery (print, paper detail, mockup), title and artist, size and presentation selector (fine art paper / acrylic / framed), price and edition counter, Add to Cart, secondary "Need more than 5 copies? Request B2B price", tabs (Description, Materials and Longevity, Artist, Shipping and Returns). Special pages: limited-editions, artists, bestsellers, new-arrivals.

Print Lab: separate language and structure. Hub hero "Professional Fine Art Printing" with CTAs "Upload your files" and "Request a quote". Three audience cards (Artists and Photographers, Designers and Agencies, Business and B2B), materials grid, "How it works" (upload, we check colour and resolution, we print and finish, you approve or we ship), CTAs (Order Samples, Request a Quote, Contact the Lab). Subpages: artists (editions, certificates, colour control, quote form), designers (custom formats, framing, interior projects), b2b (volume tiers 10 / 25 / 50+, neutral packaging, company invoicing, detailed quote form), materials (table: paper, weight, finish, longevity, recommended for, Order Sample Pack), samples (fixed-price sample pack plus first-order voucher).

## 7. Cross-cutting elements

Sample Pack (from both zones); Request a Quote (one form with "I am ordering as: Artist / Designer / Business / Collector"); shared Materials and Quality page; Certificate of Authenticity for Shop editions and optionally for the lab.

## 8. Operational note

No automated fulfillment. All print orders, from both the Shop and the Print Lab, are processed manually and on demand. The Sample Pack is the best conversion tool for first-time buyers.

Clear first-second distinction: want to buy art, go to Shop; want to print your own files or order volume, go to Print Lab.
