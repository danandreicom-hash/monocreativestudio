# Reference analysis — three homepages (observed 2026-10-18)

- Status: INPUT for TASK-002, TASK-003, TASK-005. Not a specification.
- Method: homepage text read through a page-to-text tool; typography and colour read from the live pages' computed styles in a browser. Homepages only; inner pages may differ. Messages are paraphrased. Nothing here is copied into MONO copy, and no reference is named in public copy (D-005).
- Reliability: every superlative, award, count and guarantee below is the site's own claim, unverified. Two figures on one site contradicted each other. MONO claims come only from `project/lab-facts.md` (D-002).

## 1. theprintspace

**Structure:** mega-menu, hero with one promise and one CTA, printing cards, print-on-demand block with a brand case, agency block, six-tile "why us" grid, production locations, carbon neutrality, sample pack, client logos and reviews, newsletter, news, footer.
**Messages:** speed, colour accuracy, a satisfaction guarantee, local production, carbon-neutral fulfilment, and a partner role for artists (not only a printer).
**Voice:** confident, superlative-heavy, friendly; claims mostly unquantified; headline pairs quality with speed or promises a result.
**CTAs:** generic and short (order now, learn more).
**Sample pack:** a low-priced multi-paper pack is a main conversion device.
**Type and colour observed:** one geometric humanist sans (Plus Jakarta Sans) in all roles; headlines 700 at about 40 and 28 px, body 16 px; black on white; square, unrounded controls. Plain and functional, image-led.
**Takes for MONO:** sample pack as first step; a "why us" grid of concrete benefits; a visible "how it works".
**Avoids:** unquantified superlatives, urgency, conflicting numbers.

## 2. YellowKorner

**Structure:** utility bar (find a gallery, sign in, tracking, service), mega-menu by subject and editorial areas, rotating banners, product tiles with artist links and wishlist, themed collections, "trending talents", four value pillars (concept, lab, artist support, curation), trust strip (returns, delivery, secure payment, reviews), footer taxonomy by theme, room, mood and photographer.
**Messages:** accessible limited-edition art photography; the photo as a full decor solution; curation and discovery; support for artists; low-friction buying.
**Voice:** warm, aspirational, short sentences, little jargon; benefit-led headlines that place the picture in a home or project.
**CTAs:** one recurring verb ("Discover") on every banner; browse-style secondary actions; no hard sell on the homepage.
**Mechanics:** numbered series, a "last copies" category for scarcity, best-seller and favourites lists, wishlists, gift cards, B2B and "open a gallery" links in the footer. Edition size and certificate are not shown on the homepage.
**Type and colour observed:** an editorial serif for section titles (Arizona, 32 px, regular, slightly tracked) over a neutral sans (Helvetica Neue) for body and small headings (14 to 16 px); near-black on white; small corner radius on buttons. Several decorative faces are loaded but not used on the homepage.
**Takes for MONO:** one repeated verb for the Shop; collections and artist-led entry; a serif for titles against a neutral sans; curation language; honest scarcity ("remaining") only where true.
**Avoids:** countdown discounts, banner carousels, translation leftovers.

## 3. WhiteWall

**Structure:** utility header (country and B2B switch, help, cart with an upload entry, login), product-family mega-menu, lifestyle hero with priced teasers, product tiles, award banner, core pitch with a benefits list and an upload CTA, campaign cards (limited edition, app for sellers, a flagship format, ambassadors), press logos and store locations, trust block, B2B teaser, contact options, long search-oriented text, newsletter, footer.
**Messages:** gallery-grade, award-backed output; a trusted global lab; wide choice of materials and in-house framing; personal advice, guarantees, careful packing; professional use, not only private orders.
**Voice:** premium and reassuring; headlines tie products to walls and interiors; award claims lead the hierarchy; some copy is repetitive and written for search engines.
**CTAs:** short imperatives (design, discover, upload, learn more, write to us); several contact channels shown at once.
**B2B:** a programme for professionals, resellers, designers and projects, plus a sample pack with a discount voucher.
**Type and colour observed:** system-level sans on the pages read (an icon font and a display font are loaded, but the rendered text did not show them); dark grey text on white; one warm gold used for small accent text; lifestyle photography carries the page.
**Takes for MONO:** audience-specific navigation (professionals, artists, designers); several ways to reach a person; materials taught through short, concrete lists; sample pack with a first-order voucher.
**Avoids:** award-led hierarchy MONO cannot match, SEO-style repetition, long keyword text on the homepage.

## 4. Patterns across the three

1. One promise, one primary action per block.
2. Image first; copy short; interiors and walls as context.
3. A sample pack or a trial is the entry point for new customers.
4. Professionals get their own door (agency, B2B, resellers, designers).
5. Typography is plain and neutral: either one sans, or a serif for titles with a sans for everything else. Colour is black, white and grey; the work provides the colour.
6. Trust is built with specifics (materials, locations, returns, contact channels), though each site also leans on unverified superlatives.

## 5. Direction for MONO (inputs for decision, not decisions)

- Structure: Home with two equal paths (Shop, Print Lab), a short "why MONO" grid of confirmed facts, a "for professionals" block, a sample-pack block, and a footer that repeats the audiences.
- Voice: warm and short in the Shop (collection, artist, edition), precise in the Print Lab (files, paper, colour, volume). Concrete before superlative.
- Type: a refined serif for display (Shop register and page titles) with a neutral sans for text, tables and forms; fonts self-hosted; chosen with Dan at G3 and not the same as any reference or another project of Dan's.
- Colour: neutral frame (white, warm grey, near-black); one accent from MONO's own identity if it exists.
