Cum se ambalează și se expediază printurile?

1. Flat Packaging
   Flat packaging is a simple, affordable method for smaller prints. It keeps artwork flat and prevents bending or creasing during transit. To pack your print securely, you’ll need:
   A stay-flat mailer or rigid envelope
   Two pieces of sturdy cardboard cut to the same size as your print
   A protective sleeve or glassine paper
   Tape and labels
   Slip your print into the sleeve, sandwich it between the cardboard sheets, and tape the edges together. Then place the bundle inside the mailer. This method is reliable, lightweight, and professional — perfect for smaller art reproductions and photographic prints.
   For guidance on preparing prints before shipping, visit our post on choosing the right border size.
2. Mailing Tube
   Mailing tubes are best for larger or poster-sized prints that can be rolled safely. Choose a tube slightly longer than your print’s shortest edge to avoid edge damage. Here’s how to package your print:
   Lay the artwork on craft or tissue paper and roll it carefully from the short side.
   Secure it with twine or soft tape (avoid adhesives directly on the print).
   Tuck in the paper ends to prevent dust or bending.
   Insert filler like foam or bubble wrap at both ends of the tube to keep the print from shifting.
   This method saves space, minimizes cost, and works well for durable media such as loose canvas prints.
3. Archival Bags
   Archival bags combine protection with professional presentation. Made from clear, acid-free materials like biaxially oriented polypropylene (BOPP), these sleeves safeguard your prints from moisture, fingerprints, and dust. They also give your packaging a polished, gallery-ready look.
   BOPP bags come in several styles — including protective-closure, flap-seal, and no-flap versions — making it easy to find one that matches your brand’s aesthetic. Pair them with a rigid mailer or box for extra support during shipment.
